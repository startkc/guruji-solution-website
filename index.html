IMPORTANT

Verify before you pay

Never rely only on an advertisement, agent, social media post or verbal assurance. Confirm the exact course, campus and admission session through official sources.

Visit UGC website ↗

Different programmes are regulated by different authorities. UGC information alone may not establish approval for every professional course.
